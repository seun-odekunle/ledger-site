Privacy policy

Last updated August 2026.

Ledger does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There's nothing to have a privacy policy about, other than saying so plainly.

What Ledger stores

Every habit, task, and completion you record is saved locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). None of it is sent anywhere — Ledger has no server, no analytics, and no account system of any kind.

What Ledger doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No network requests of any kind
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or third-party tracking

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Ledger schedules local notifications on your device using Apple's notification system. These never leave your device and aren't visible to anyone but you.

TestFlight

If you installed Ledger through TestFlight, Apple may collect standard installation, usage, and crash diagnostics as part of operating TestFlight itself. That data is handled under Apple's own privacy policy, separate from Ledger.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Ledger removes everything, since nothing exists outside your device. Settings also has a "Clear all data" option inside the app if you'd rather keep the app but start fresh.

Questions

Reach out any time — see the support page.
